Welcome to North Star Bakery!

What We're Known For

We keep things simple: quality ingredients, time-honored techniques, and plenty of care.

Our neighborhood bakery is known for naturally leavened breads, laminated pastries with crisp, buttery layers, seasonal sweets, and celebration cakes made to order. Everything is baked in small batches, so what comes out of the oven is fresh, thoughtful, and made to be enjoyed that day.

Local favorites include:

  • Slow-fermented sourdough with a crackling crust
  • Buttery croissants baked fresh each morning
  • Seasonal fruit danishes and morning buns
  • Soft sandwich loaves for everyday meals
  • Custom cakes for birthdays, weddings, showers, and celebrations
  • Rotating weekend specials inspired by the season
