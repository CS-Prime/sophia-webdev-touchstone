Our Story

A Neighborhood Bakery With a Simple Idea

North Star Bakery started with a belief that a neighborhood feels more like home when there’s a place where people know your name—and know how you take your coffee. What began with a few sourdough loaves, a small mixer, and a very early alarm clock grew into a bakery built around the rhythm of our community. We wanted to create the kind of place you could stop by on your morning commute, visit with your kids on Saturday, or call when you needed a cake for someone you love. A place where the bread is still warm, the pastry case changes with the seasons, and there’s usually something new coming out of the oven. Today, we still make everything in small batches, still shape our breads by hand, and still get excited when a regular discovers a new favorite. Because to us, a bakery isn’t just about what we make. It’s about who we get to make it for.

Our Ingredients

Good Baking Starts With Good Ingredients

We believe great food doesn’t need to be complicated. It just needs to begin with ingredients worth caring about. Whenever possible, we work with local and regional producers and choose ingredients for flavor, quality, and responsible sourcing.

That means:

  • Flour selected for quality and consistency
  • Cultured butter for richer, more flavorful pastries
  • Local eggs when available
  • Seasonal fruits sourced close to home whenever possible
  • Real chocolate, vanilla, spices, and natural flavorings
  • Fresh herbs and produce from regional growers
  • No unnecessary shortcuts

Our menu follows the seasons, which means a strawberry tart in June might become an apple galette in October. We think that’s part of the fun. We bake with what tastes good now.

Meet the North Star Team

The People Behind the Pastry Case

North Star Bakery is powered by early mornings, flour-dusted aprons, strong coffee, and a team of people who genuinely love making food for others.

Emma — Founder & Head Baker

Emma started North Star after years spent baking professionally and dreaming about opening a shop of her own. She’s happiest working with bread dough before sunrise and still personally checks the first sourdough loaves out of the oven each morning.

Favorite bake: Country sourdough with salted butter.

Mateo — Pastry Lead

Mateo is the reason our croissants have so many layers. With an eye for detail and a love of seasonal ingredients, he develops many of the pastries and weekend specials you’ll find in our case.

Favorite bake: Almond croissant, preferably while still warm.

Claire — Cake Decorator

From understated birthday cakes to elaborate wedding designs, Claire brings celebrations to life with buttercream, flowers, piping, and an impressive amount of patience.

Favorite bake: Lemon cake with raspberry filling.

Jamie — Front of House

Jamie knows our regulars, remembers their usual orders, and can probably tell you which pastry you should try before you’ve decided yourself.

Favorite bake: Whatever just came out of the oven.
